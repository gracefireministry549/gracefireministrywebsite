About Us

Grace, Fire & Healing for Every Nation

Our Mission

Raising a Family of Believers

Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry exists to bring the grace, fire and healing power of the gospel to Isheri Osun, Lagos, and to the nations of the world. We are committed to sound teaching of the Word, fervent prayer, deliverance ministration and divine healing.

We believe every soul deserves an encounter with the living God — one that sets captives free, restores broken lives and establishes families in grace.

“I Am a Product of Grace — If You Try Me, My Grace Will Disgrace You.”

The Man of God

Pastor Ifeanyi Ude

Pastor Ifeanyi Ude is the founder and lead pastor of Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry. Under his leadership, the ministry has grown into a house of prayer known for deliverance, healing and the demonstration of the power of God.

He is passionate about equipping believers with the Word of God, and his ministration under the anointing has brought healing and freedom to many, in fulfilment of Hebrews 12:2 — the scripture that anchors this ministry.
