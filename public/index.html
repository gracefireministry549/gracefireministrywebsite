30 August 2026 · Nine Years of Grace, Fire & Healing

To God Be All the Glory!

Our 9th Church Anniversary was a success, and our hearts are full of gratitude. Thank You, Heavenly Father, for Your grace, protection, provision, healing, and for every life You have touched through this ministry.

To every member, guest, friend, partner, and well-wisher who prayed, served, gave, and celebrated with us: thank you. May the Lord bless you richly. We give Him all the praise for nine years of faithfulness and trust Him for the years ahead.
