Donation Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how visitors may support the work of Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry through the partnership information published on this website.

1. Voluntary support

Any gifts, offerings or partnership contributions are voluntary. Please do not give money you need for essential living expenses, and do not feel pressured to contribute. A contribution is not a payment for prayer, healing, deliverance, pastoral attention or a promised spiritual outcome.

2. Verify details before sending

Before making a transfer, confirm the current account name, bank and account number using the ministry’s official contact details. Do not rely on a forwarded message, an unsolicited request, or details that appear to have been changed. If anything seems uncertain, contact the ministry first.

3. Receipts and records

Keep your transfer confirmation for your own records. If you need the ministry to confirm receipt, contact us and provide only the transaction details reasonably needed to trace the contribution. Never send your PIN, password, one-time passcode or full card details.

4. Refunds and corrections

If you believe a contribution was sent in error, contact the ministry promptly with the date, amount and transaction reference. We will review the information and respond. A refund is not automatic and may depend on whether the transfer can be verified, recovered and lawfully returned.

5. Use of contributions

Contributions support ministry activities and related operating needs. The ministry will use funds in accordance with its purposes and applicable requirements. This website does not currently promise a specific allocation or a tax-deductible status for contributions.

6. No third-party payment guarantee

Where you use a bank, payment provider or other third-party service, that provider’s terms and processes also apply. The ministry does not request confidential banking credentials.

7. Questions

For partnership questions or to verify contribution details, contact gracefireministry1@gmail.com or call 080-54-30-22-47.

Questions or privacy requests?

Contact Grace Fire Ministry at gracefireministry1@gmail.com or call 080-54-30-22-47. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.
