Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grace Fire Deliverance Healing Global World Ministry (“Grace Fire Ministry”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website, contact the ministry, or submit a prayer request. It is written for our current website features and should be updated if those features or service providers change.

1. Information you choose to provide

  • Prayer requests: your name, and any phone number, email address or prayer details you choose to submit.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address, subject and message.
  • Direct contact: information you send when you call, email or contact us through WhatsApp.
  • Public contributions: if you voluntarily send a testimony, photograph or other material for publication, we receive the material and any details you provide with it.

2. Sensitive and personal information

Prayer requests may contain sensitive personal details, including information about faith, family circumstances or health. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing. Do not include bank details, passwords, government identification numbers or information about another person unless you have a proper reason and permission to share it.

3. How we use information

  • To receive and respond to prayer requests and general enquiries.
  • To contact you when you ask us to follow up.
  • To coordinate ministry communication and activities.
  • To review website submissions, prevent misuse and maintain website security.
  • To publish a testimony or other contribution only where appropriate permission has been obtained.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use prayer requests as public website content.

4. Where information is processed

Website forms are designed to submit information to our hosted database service, Supabase. Website hosting is provided through Render. The site also links to or loads services such as Google Maps, Google Fonts and WhatsApp when you choose to use those features. Those providers may process technical information under their own privacy terms. We do not control their independent practices.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We limit access to submitted information to people who need it for ministry administration or to respond to you. We may use service providers to host and operate the website. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect people, the ministry or the security of the website. We do not make prayer requests available to the general public.

6. Retention and security

We keep submitted information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, ministry administration, resolving enquiries, security or applicable legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information you have submitted, request correction, or ask us to delete it where we are able and legally permitted to do so. We may need to verify that the request is yours before acting. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate or legal reasons.

8. Children and young people

We encourage children and young people to involve a parent or trusted guardian before submitting personal or sensitive information online. A parent or guardian may contact us with a privacy concern about a young person’s submission.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently advertise an account-based tracking or analytics programme. Hosting, embedded maps, fonts or other third-party services may use technical storage or collect connection information according to their own settings and policies. You can manage cookies through your browser. Blocking some third-party features may affect how they work.

10. External links and policy updates

Links to WhatsApp, maps and other external websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We may update this notice when the website or our practices change. The updated date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Questions or privacy requests?

Contact Grace Fire Ministry at gracefireministry1@gmail.com or call 080-54-30-22-47. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.
